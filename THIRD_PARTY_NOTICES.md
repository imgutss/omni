# Third-party rights

Omni Panel includes a preserved bundled backend, client-format templates, provider integrations and dependencies. Existing embedded attributions and applicable upstream rights remain in force. Package licenses are identified by the lockfile and each package's distribution; third-party rule feeds, artwork references and client formats retain their own terms.

This repository does not invent a new blanket license for pre-existing bundled code or relicense the separate Android app. Public source availability alone is not a universal redistribution grant. Review the relevant rights before redistributing under a chosen license.
